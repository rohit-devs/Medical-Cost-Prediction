Cost factors

What usually moves the price

These are the inputs the estimator looks at. In many insurance datasets the effect of each one is not equal.

Age

Costs generally rise as people get older, because medical needs increase over time.

BMI

Body mass index is a common risk indicator that insurers and models use as a signal.

Smoking

Smoking status is often one of the strongest drivers of predicted cost in this kind of data.

Family & region

Number of children and where you live can shift the result by a smaller margin.

FAQ

Quick answers

How accurate is the estimate?

The figure comes from a machine learning model trained on sample insurance records. Treat it as a planning guide, not a quote. Real premiums depend on the insurer, the plan and your full medical history.

What is BMI and how do I find mine?

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres squared. Use the "Calculate it" link under the BMI field and it will fill in for you.

Does the page remember what I typed?

Your entries are kept only in this browser tab so the form can be pre-filled if you come back to adjust them. Use "Reset form" to clear them at any time.

Why do you ask for gender and region?

They are two of the inputs the model was trained with. They are used only to produce the estimate shown on the next screen.
